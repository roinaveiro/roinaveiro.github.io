## Background

I obtained my PhD in Machine Learning, Statistics, and Operations Research at [ICMAT-CSIC](https://www.icmat.es/), supervised by [David Ríos Insua](https://www.icmat.es/drios). I have also been a visiting scholar at Duke University and SAMSI.

## Awards and grants

- **2026** · [Leonardo Grant, BBVA Foundation](https://www.redleonardo.es/beneficiario/roi-naveiro-flores/), for the project *LUAR: Aprendizaje bajo riesgos adversarios*.
- **2026** · [SEIO–BBVA Foundation Award](https://www.fbbva.es/noticias/fallados-premios-estadistica-seio-fundacion-bbva-2026/) for the best contribution to Data Science from Statistics and Operations Research, jointly with David Ríos Insua, Víctor Gallego, and Jason Poulos.
- **2023** · [SEIO–BBVA Foundation Award](https://www.fbbva.es/galardonados/roi-naveiro-flores-tahir-ekin-david-rios-insua-y-alberto-torres-barran/) for the best methodological contribution in Operations Research, jointly with Tahir Ekin, David Ríos Insua, and Alberto Torres-Barrán.
- **2022** · CSIC award for the best doctoral thesis in basic AI research.

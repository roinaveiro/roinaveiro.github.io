## Background

I obtained my PhD in Machine Learning, Statistics, and Operations Research at [ICMAT-CSIC](https://www.icmat.es/), supervised by [David Ríos Insua](https://www.icmat.es/drios). I have also been a visiting scholar at Duke University and SAMSI.

My work received the CSIC award for the best thesis in basic AI research in 2022, and the BBVA Foundation–SEIO award for the best methodological contribution in Operations Research in 2024.

## Talks and teaching

My latest lecture, [Secure Machine Learning: a Bayesian perspective](https://roinaveiro.github.io/talks/cristalera/secure-ml.html), covers adversarial attacks and defenses, sequential decisions, and AI opponents.
